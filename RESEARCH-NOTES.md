## 2. Verified positions and activities

### Khandesh Sahitya Sangh (खान्देश साहित्य संघ)
- Described as **National / Central President** (राष्ट्रीय अध्यक्ष / केंद्रीय अध्यक्ष).
- In August 2021 he announced the organisation's **first multilingual literary conference** at Jalgaon.
- The conference was to include Ahirani and sister dialects: अहिराणी, बागलाणी, नेमाडी, लाडशिक्की, पावरी, भिलाऊ, भावसारी, लेवा पाटीदारी, गुजराऊ, तडवी, डांगी, पावरा, कोकणी, नंदुरबारी, उर्दू, हिंदी, मराठी.
- He has presided over Khandesh Sahitya Sangh meetings (e.g., at Pratap College, Amalner, January 2026) and announced appointments, including the state president of the **Saneguruji Shikshak Sahitya Sangh**.
- Quoted on the need to sustain the literary movement and give the new generation a platform.

### Ahirani literary movement
- Associated with the **Akhil Bharatiya Ahirani Sahitya Sammelan** (अखिल भारतीय अहिराणी साहित्य संमेलन).
- Featured in connection with the **6th Akhil Bharatiya Ahirani Sahitya Sammelan, Dhule**.
- Publicly argues that imposing Hindi amounts to disrespecting dialects, and speaks for the protection and promotion of Marathi and Maharashtra's dialects.
- Writes and is described as a **Marathi ghazal** writer; his ghazals are shared on social media.

### Maratha Seva Sangh
- Spoke at the **36th anniversary lecture** of the Maratha Seva Sangh, Dhule district office (reported September 2026).
- Topic: **"मराठा सेवा संघ: काल, आज, उद्या"**.
- Reported statements: the Maratha identity is a thought/ideology, not merely a caste; the organisation promotes freedom, justice, equality, fraternity and social democracy; founded 1 September 1990; works through 33 "प्रबोधन कक्ष".

### Media / public discourse
- YouTube talks include:
  - "हिंदी सक्ती म्हणजे बोलीभाषांना कमी लेखणे आहे — प्रा. सदाशिव सुर्यवंशी"
  - "मराठी भाषेचा जागर मायमराठी साठी केलाच पाहिजे — प्रा. सदाशिव सुर्यवंशी"
  - "आंतरराज्य अहिराणी संमेलन सोनगड — डॉ. सदाशिव सुर्यवंशी, धुळे"
- A Rashtramat article on Ahirani language references him in the context of Ahirani literary organising (January 2024).

## 6. Content-writing guidance for Marathi

- Write in natural, standard Marathi; avoid literal English translation.
- Use `।` for Marathi sentence endings where natural.
- Keep honourifics: प्रा., डॉ., ताई, सर, सौ.
- For Ahirani, preserve the Khandesh dialect's flavour; do not "correct" it into standard Marathi.
- Poems: preserve line breaks, avoid justification, keep a comfortable measure.
- Add an English one-line summary for important pages if the owner wants non-Marathi readers.
- Attribute every news item with `source_name` and `source_url`.
